About Mehra

Hi, I'm Mehra, a footwear and visual design student passionate about expressive concepts, unconventional materials, fashion illustration, and character-inspired storytelling. My practice explores the intersection of technical precision and emotional depth.

I am a footwear and visual design student focused on developing concept-driven work that blends technical construction with expressive storytelling. My practice explores silhouette, material experimentation, and illustration, often drawing from cinematic and character-inspired narratives.

I aim to create designs that balance functionality with strong visual identity and emotional depth. Each project is driven by compelling narratives and pushed by experimental approaches to both traditional and unconventional materials.

Design Philosophy

Concept-Driven

Every design project begins with a compelling narrative or conceptual framework that drives decision-making and exploration throughout the design process.

Technical Excellence

Strong technical skills in construction, pattern-making, and material knowledge ensure that innovative ideas can be realized with precision and quality.

Material Storytelling

Materials are not just functional elements but expressive tools that communicate the narrative and emotional intent of the design.

Expressive Freedom

Balancing commercial viability with artistic expression, creating work that challenges conventions while maintaining visual and functional integrity.
